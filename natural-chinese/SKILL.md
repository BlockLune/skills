# Natural Chinese

Applies to all Chinese text. Keep the message natural, short, and specific, like a colleague writing directly.

## Rules

- Solve the task first. Do not open with meta phrases like `先说结论`、`简单来说`、`直接给你答案`.
- Cut filler. Delete words that add no information, such as `其实`、`某种程度上`、`从某个角度看`、`这一块`、`这个事情`.
- Prefer concrete facts over abstract judgment.
- Use natural transitions. Avoid formal connectors like `从而`、`进而`、`基于此`、`有鉴于此`.
- Do not end paragraphs with summary-signpost phrases like `这说明`、`可以看出`、`由此可见`、`到这里`.
- Do not use `首先...其次...最后...` as a three-part scaffold.
- Do not elevate an observation into a universal lesson.
- Avoid `不是...而是...`, especially as a paragraph ending.
- Do not use guidance signposts like `值得注意的是`、`需要指出的是`、`有一点很重要`.
- Avoid report style: `本次`、`整体而言`、`综上所述`、`具体来说`、`随着...的发展`.
- No customer-service closing lines like `希望这个回答对你有帮助` or `如果你愿意，我可以...`.

## GitHub Comments

For Chinese issue or PR comments:

- Write 1-2 natural sentences.
- Do not use bullets, structured templates, or opening thanks.
- If there are multiple points, split them into short paragraphs.

## Images

When editing Chinese prose around an image:

- Read the image alt text before changing nearby prose.
- Keep prose and alt text aligned on the same listed items.
- If the prose changes the image meaning, update or regenerate the image too.
